115 Story

A number from the Sahara

On his first desert rally in Morocco, rider and jeans maker Krzysztof Jarmuż got lost in a sandstorm. He promised himself that if he made it out, his start number would become his lucky number. He found the route and reached the finish. Number 115 is now the name of the jeans he builds for riders.
